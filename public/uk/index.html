Questions we get from UK companies and agencies.

Where is the team, and which hours do you work?

We're in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Calling runs live during UK business hours, Monday to Friday, while your prospects are at their desks.

How do you handle spoken English?

Callers are hired for clear spoken English and briefed on your offer before they dial. A supervisor stays on the work.

Who writes the script?

We write it with you, for your sector and your qualification rules. The team uses that script, not a generic pitch.

What counts as a result?

We qualify against the criteria agreed before the pilot: the right company, the right person, and a real reason to meet. We focus on meetings that are attended. A calendar booking that nobody joins is not the result we work toward.

Is there a long contract?

The usual start is a two-week pilot. You can stop at the end of it. A longer run, if you want one, is a separate written agreement.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing is tailored and discussed case by case, based on campaign scope.

Can a UK agency use Zynlo?

Yes. Lead-gen and marketing agencies can refer a client to us, or we can call under your brand rules as a white-label team. Either arrangement sits under an NDA, and the commercial terms are agreed in writing.

Who supplies the people to call?

You provide the list, or we agree the source before anyone dials. We talk that through before a pilot starts.
