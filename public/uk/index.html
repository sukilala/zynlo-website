Questions UK companies and agencies ask first.

The commercial details, in plain language.

Where is the team, and which hours do you work?
The team is in Colombo and dials live during UK business hours, Monday to Friday, while your prospects are at their desks.
How do you handle spoken English?
Callers are hired for clear spoken English and briefed on your offer before they dial. A supervisor stays on the work.
Who writes the script?
We write it with you, for your sector and your qualification rules. The team uses that script.
What counts as a result?
We qualify against the criteria agreed before the pilot: the right company, the right person, and a real reason to meet. The result we work toward is a meeting that is attended.
Is there a long contract?
The usual start is a two-week pilot. You can stop at the end of it. A longer run is a separate written agreement.
How is pricing decided?
Pricing is tailored and discussed case by case, based on campaign scope. There is no public rate card.
Can a UK agency use Zynlo?
Yes. Lead-gen and marketing agencies can refer a client, or we can call under your brand rules as a white-label team. Either way sits under an NDA, and the commercial terms are agreed in writing.
Who supplies the people to call?
You provide the list, or we agree the source before anyone dials.
