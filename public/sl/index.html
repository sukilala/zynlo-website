Questions from Sri Lankan operators.

Do you cover nights, weekends, and Poya days?

Messaging and Voice can run around the clock, including weekends and Poya days. Sunday and Sri Lankan public holidays, Poya included, are billed at 1.5× the usual rate. A Dedicated Seat is a set shift: 8 hours a day, 6 days a week.

What is the Rs. 39 messaging window?

The Messaging plan is Rs. 39 per window, not per message. A window is up to 8 hours of conversation with one customer on WhatsApp, email, or live chat. Everything inside that block is one charge. A new message from the same customer after the window ends starts another window.

Is this the WhatsApp Business API or our existing number?

We work with your existing WhatsApp Business setup and follow WhatsApp's rules for business messaging. You grant secure access. We keep the number off unapproved automation.

What about EPF, ETF, and hiring?

The people are employed and supervised by Zynlo. They are not added to your payroll, so you don't run EPF or ETF for them, and you don't recruit them yourself.

How is a Dedicated Seat different from 24/7 cover?

Messaging and Voice are the plans for coverage beyond one shift, including evenings. A Dedicated Seat is one person on your brand for 8 hours a day, 6 days a week, on the hours you agree. Hours outside that shift are quoted separately.

What is a Lost Revenue Audit?

We look through your last 100 WhatsApp chats and calls and point out where replies were missed or slow, so you can judge whether always-on cover is worth it. It is free. Sending the form does not create a contract.

How soon can we start?

We start once you have shared access and how the brand should sound. The timing follows that handover.

Which plan should we start with?

Messaging if most demand is WhatsApp, email, or live chat. Voice if orders and complaints still come in by phone. A Dedicated Seat if volume is daily and you want one person fully on your brand.
