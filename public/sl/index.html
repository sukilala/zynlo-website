Frequently asked questions

Practical answers for Sri Lankan operators evaluating always-on WhatsApp and call coverage.

Do you cover Poya days and public holidays?
Yes. Zynlo is calendar-aware for Avurudu, Wesak, Poya days, and other public holidays. Sunday and holiday coverage is available at 1.5× rate so your customers never hit a dead line. A Dedicated Seat is a set shift: 8 hours a day, 6 days a week. Hours outside that shift are quoted separately.
What is the Rs. 39 messaging window?
The Messaging plan is Rs. 39 per window, not per message. A window is up to 8 hours of conversation with one customer on WhatsApp, email, or live chat. Everything inside that block is one charge. A new message from the same customer after the window ends starts another window.
Is this WhatsApp Business API or my existing number?
We work with your existing WhatsApp Business setup and stay Meta-compliant. You grant secure access. We never risk your number or reputation with unapproved automation.
What about EPF, ETF, and HR risk?
Zero. Agents are managed by Zynlo. You get service outcomes without payroll, EPF/ETF liabilities, absenteeism, or hiring delays.
How is a Dedicated Seat different from 24/7 cover?
Messaging and Voice are the plans for coverage beyond one shift, including evenings, weekends, and Poya days. A Dedicated Seat is one person on your brand for 8 hours a day, 6 days a week. Hours outside that shift are quoted separately.
What is a Lost Revenue Audit?
Send us your number. We review your last 100 chats and calls, estimate missed or delayed responses, and show the revenue impact of going always-on. Free, no obligation.
How fast can we go live?
Most clients start within 48 hours after access and brand guidelines are shared. Dedicated seats include training on your products, tone, and escalation paths.
Which plan should I start with?
Messaging suits chat-heavy brands validating demand. Voice fits phone-first order taking. Dedicated Seat is for predictable daily volume where you want one agent fully focused on your brand.
