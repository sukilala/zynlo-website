Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026. This policy is written for our work in Sri Lanka and the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (the PDPA).

Zynlo Technologies (PVT) LTD ("Zynlo", "we") runs www.zynlo.lk and provides a human team that answers WhatsApp messages and phone calls for businesses. This page says what personal data that involves, why we use it, and how to reach us.

Who is responsible

Zynlo Technologies (PVT) LTD, a company in Sri Lanka, is the organisation responsible for personal data described here. For customer messages and calls we handle on a client's behalf, the client decides why that data is used and we follow the client's instructions and our non-disclosure agreement.

The audit form on this website

The "Request your free audit" form asks for three things:

  • your name
  • your phone number (the WhatsApp number you want us to use)
  • your industry

The form does not send those details to a Zynlo database. When you submit it, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message that already includes your name, number, and industry, addressed to +94 72 358 9231. You can edit or close that message before you send it. If you send it, we receive it as a WhatsApp chat.

We use that message to reply about a free Lost Revenue Audit and to talk about whether our WhatsApp or call team is a fit. We do not sell these details. We do not add you to a marketing list you did not ask for.

WhatsApp and Meta

WhatsApp is operated by Meta. When you send the audit message, and when support later runs on WhatsApp, Meta processes the conversation under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy notice. That can include processing outside Sri Lanka. We only receive what is in the chat. We do not control Meta's systems.

Calls are carried on the telephone network the client uses. We handle the conversation so we can answer the caller for that business.

Client operations, under NDA

When a business engages Zynlo, our team uses that business's WhatsApp, phone, and related tools to answer its customers. The names, numbers, orders, and other details in those conversations are the client's customer data. We handle them only to do the work the client asked for, under a non-disclosure agreement. We do not sell that data, and we do not use a client's customers as a list to promote Zynlo.

The client is responsible for having the right to share that data with us, and for telling its own customers what the law requires.

Cookies and similar tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing of that kind to accept or refuse.

The site is served through Cloudflare. Delivering a page can create ordinary connection logs, such as IP address, time, and the address of the page. Those logs are used to run and protect the site, not to build a marketing profile of you.

Why we are allowed to use personal data

In plain terms:

  • You send the audit details so we can answer you. That request is why we use them.
  • Client operations data is used because of the contract with the business and the NDA.
  • If a Sri Lankan law requires us to keep a record, we keep it for that reason.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it. Withdrawing consent does not undo a message already sent through WhatsApp.

How long we keep it

We keep an audit enquiry for as long as we need to answer it and any follow-up you ask for. If we do not start working together, we delete those details once the conversation is finished, unless the law requires a record. Client operations data is kept for the time set out in the client agreement and the NDA, and is then deleted or returned.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only as far as the work requires:

  • Meta, when the message or the support conversation is on WhatsApp
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website
  • the business client, for the customers we are supporting on that client's behalf
  • a public authority, if Sri Lankan law requires it

We do not sell personal data.

Your rights under the PDPA

The Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 gives people rights over their personal data. You can ask us:

  • to confirm whether we process personal data about you, and to give you access to it
  • to correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • to erase personal data, or to stop a particular use, where the Act allows it
  • to withdraw consent, where we are relying on consent
  • to object to processing, where the Act allows it

Email hello@zynlo.lk or message +94 72 358 9231. We may ask for enough information to confirm the request is yours before we act on it.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka, the regulator under the Act.

Security

Access to a client's tools is limited to the people doing that client's work, and the work sits under an NDA. No message service is a locked box: once a chat is in WhatsApp, Meta processes it as well.

Children

This site is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the form.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
